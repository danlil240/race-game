Text, translations and audio
-----------------------------
The English/Hebrew text tables (compiled into the player from
Assets/FamilyRacing/Core/Localization) and every .wav cue are original
work produced for this project; no third-party text or voice asset
ships. Costs recorded in docs/ASSET_INVENTORY.md remain US$0.

Signature status
----------------
The Windows player is NOT Authenticode-signed (no paid code-signing
certificate is part of this private family release). The Android
package is signed with the family release key whose SHA-256
certificate fingerprint is recorded in release-manifest.json.
