# Sunny Island asset trial

The latest requested world pass is now the **three-location closed-circuit
build**. See [CLOSED_CIRCUITS.md](CLOSED_CIRCUITS.md) and launch
`Builds/Windows-Circuits/FamilyRacing.exe`. The sections below also describe the
earlier graphics and Starter Cup passes retained for comparison.

This is an opt-in art prototype for the approved free Kenney car + Quaternius
scenery comparison. It is not the production art rollout.

## Run the trial

1. Open this project with Unity **6000.3.26f1** (license rechecked successfully).
2. Let the source models and textures import.
3. Choose **Family Racing → Asset Trial → Create Sunny Island Trial**.
4. The utility saves and opens
   `Assets/FamilyRacing/Generated/AssetTrial/SunnyIslandAssetTrial.unity`.
5. Press Play, choose practice and the car. Compare the opening straight and the
   north-east island garden in chase and overhead cameras.

A ready Windows development player is at
`Builds/Windows-AssetTrial/FamilyRacing.exe`. Launch with `-force-d3d11` for
the graphics backend used for the screenshots. Its save data is isolated when
launched with `--validation-dir <new-empty-directory>`; ordinary launches use
the existing game's normal profile location.

Art study image: `docs/qa/asset-trial-2026-10-23/staged-art-preview.png`.
This is a staged Unity render of the real assets, with the driving HUD excluded.
The `screenshot-asset-trial-*.png` files in that directory are actual gameplay.

The utility clones the existing benchmark course, car definition and scene into
the trial folder. Its scene uses content version `asset-trial-2` and is not added
to production build settings. Original car/scene/track assets are not regenerated.
The existing driving rig, collision and route remain in use. The imported Kenney
chassis now has profile-colored paint, rounded treaded tires, alloy rims, lights,
a bucket seat, roll hoop, steering wheel and racing stripes. Added scenery has
camera-only collision on Ignore Raycast, excluded by driving physics.

## Starting Campaign

The original graphics-only trial inherited an empty `gameplayCatalog` from the
benchmark scene, so Campaign, Quick Race and Time Trial correctly reported
`contentUnavailable`. Version `asset-trial-2` binds its own validated Starter Cup
catalog in `Generated/AssetTrial/Campaign`.

Launch the updated Windows player, select/create a profile, open **Campaign**,
select an event, choose its car or motorcycle, then scroll to **Race**. The cup
has four regular events on the existing coastal course (one circuit and one
two-lap time challenge per class). Completing all four with six regional stars
opens the two class finales. This is a short playable campaign on one course,
not the planned multi-world adventure. Practice stays available.

Finishes award 20 completion coins plus placement and earned star bonuses through
the existing persisted reward transaction. The catalog includes the three
standard upgrade categories, three beginner bounties, gadgets and three AI
difficulty settings. Trial world/event IDs are distinct from future production
content. Normal profiles are not reset by rebuilding.

`AssetTrialCampaignCheck.py` reproduces the missing-content failure on the old
player, then checks campaign access, locked finale rejection, first-race finish,
coins/stars, Continue, all four starter-event starts and progress after a full
player restart. All validation runs use isolated synthetic profile stores.

## Car and animal pass

All four trial animals (fox, rabbit, panda and raccoon) have original authored
faces, species-specific ears and tails, aviator goggles, racing suits, gloves,
boots and profile-colored scarves. Their named bones still use the existing
seat, hand aim, head turn, lean and celebration rig on both cars and motorcycles.
`AssetTrialRacerLife` adds blinking, gentle ear/tail movement and scarf flutter
on separate child transforms; it never writes simulation state. Static detail
is combined per material and articulated node to limit renderers.

The trial scene references its own character definitions in
`Generated/AssetTrial/Racers`. Production character assets remain separate.
The rounded tire mesh is authored along the spin node's Y axis so the driving
rig's axle rotation is applied once. The car paint is split from the imported
palette mesh, preserving its existing engine and trim colors.

Reproducible studio previews are generated by
`FamilyRacing.EditorTools.AssetTrialRacers.Preview` and included in
`CreateBuildPreview`. Images are in
`docs/qa/asset-trial-2026-10-23/racer-pass/`. They are staged Unity renders,
not gameplay captures. Actual gameplay coverage uses
`python Tools/AssetTrialRacerShots.py <run>/connection.json --evidence <new-dir>`:
four animals × two vehicle types × chase/overhead cameras, with streamed
identity assertions. No additional third-party files or paid assets were added
for this pass. Tablet performance still requires physical-device verification.

## Coastal scenery pass

`AssetTrialCoast` replaces the four village house visuals with detailed Kenney
models while keeping the original island collision and camera occluders. Boats,
wooden docks and buoys sit beyond the existing sea walls. Four distant rock
islands with trees add a coastal skyline. Alternating navy/cream paint along the
long road sections is batched into ten small meshes, with no added colliders.

Two original URP shaders add opaque animated water and a gradient sky with
slow-moving cloud patches. Neither shader uses transparency, real-time
reflections, depth grabs or post-processing. The trial chase camera is slightly
lower (3.25 m rise, 8.5 m back) to expose more scenery and horizon. The overhead
camera keeps its existing settings.

The source east/west sand skirts incorrectly swapped their axes before rotating,
producing a 59.7 m tall sand slope behind the harbor. The trial now authors these
skirts in a consistent local basis with a shallow outward slope. A new validation
guard first failed on the old geometry (`Logs/asset-trial-shoreline-red.log`) and
passes after the correction. Original course geometry is untouched; these skirts
are renderer-only objects.

Latest build: `Logs/asset-trial-coastal-final-build.log` (zero errors). Collision and
material validation passed. The English car tour covers opening, garden, east
harbor and southern village, with both cameras plus pause/resume and restart.
The second run checks the motorcycle in Hebrew at 1024×768. Evidence folders:
`Logs/asset-trial-coastal-final-tour/art-evidence/` and
`Logs/asset-trial-coastal-final-moto-he/art-evidence/`. Saved captures are under
`docs/qa/asset-trial-2026-10-23/coastal-pass/`.

The third-party manifest now records 32 source files across four source packs.
All new shaders, edge meshes and surface textures are original project work.
Physical A7 Lite performance and on-device visibility remain unverified; this
pass is ready for PC review, not a claim of tablet qualification.

To reproduce the extended car capture, pass `--coastal-tour` to
